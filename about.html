My Story

Short Biography

I'm Bryant Duhart — a digital media designer and the founder of Shimti Multimedia, working internationally across design, video, 3D, music, and interactive media.

I've been a working creative since 2006, building my craft as a freelancer while holding down the operational side of business in visual merchandising, print production management, and customer-facing roles. That parallel track shaped how I work today: creative output delivered with the punctuality, decisiveness, and accountability of an operations professional. I'm deliberately self-taught — every skill in my toolkit was earned project by project, and I direct AI as part of my production pipeline the way a studio directs its staff.

Shimti takes its name from the ancient Babylonian Bit Shimti — "the house where the breath of life is given." This is mine: a modern laboratory where ideas are given form. The portfolio shows what comes out of it; if your project deserves that standard, get in touch.
