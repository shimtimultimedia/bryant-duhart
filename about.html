Short Biography

I'm Bryant Duhart — an independent multidisciplinary creator in Wiesbaden, Germany, and founder of SHIMTI Multimedia.

My work connects creative production with technical execution: web design, visual systems, video, 3D, music and audio, photography, app prototyping, AI-assisted workflows, and game-development concepts.

My background in operations, retail management, custom apparel, visual merchandising, music/media production, freelance work, and self-directed technical learning gives me a practical view of how creative work has to function in the real world.
