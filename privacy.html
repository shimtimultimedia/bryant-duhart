The United Kingdom is covered by an EU adequacy decision. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in showing where the studio is based). You can avoid this by not opening the contact page; the email address is also listed in the Legal Notice. Details: OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps before a contract) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering general questions). Details: Google Privacy Policy.
