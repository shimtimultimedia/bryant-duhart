Bryant Duhart

Portfolio

Bryant Duhart is a multidisciplinary digital media and technology specialist. His work spans design — web, graphic, and print; motion and sound — video and media workflows and music production; and build — 3D modelling, game development, software prototyping, AI-assisted creative processes, and interactive visual concepts.

As the founder of Shimti Multimedia, he develops practical creative systems for digital publishing, visual storytelling, online presence, and multimedia production. His background spans customer-facing operations, retail management, custom apparel production, visual merchandising, freelance work, entrepreneurship, and self-directed technical learning.

His approach is project-led: each engagement is matched to the medium, toolchain, and platform best suited to it, with the operational, commercial, and technical sides handled by the same hand from first sketch to final delivery.
