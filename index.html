Portfolio

Bryant Duhart is a multidisciplinary digital media and technology specialist based in Wiesbaden, Germany. His work combines web design, graphic design, print production, video/media workflows, AI-assisted creative processes, software prototyping, 3D modelling, game development, music production, and interactive visual concepts.

As the founder of Shimti Multimedia, he develops practical creative systems for digital publishing, visual storytelling, online presence, and multimedia production. His background spans customer-facing operations, retail management, custom apparel production, visual merchandising, music/media creation, freelance work, entrepreneurship, and self-directed technical learning.

His approach is project-led: each engagement is matched to the medium, toolchain, and platform best suited to it, with the operational, commercial, and technical sides handled by the same hand from first sketch to final delivery. The services below outline what that covers; the portfolio gathers the finished work and the platforms it lives on.
