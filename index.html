Bryant Duhart

Portfolio

This is the personal portfolio of Bryant Duhart, a self-taught multimedia designer based in Wiesbaden, Germany, and the founder of Shimti Multimedia. Where the studio's own site presents the company, this one presents the person behind the work.

It is written for two kinds of visitor: clients looking for someone to design, film, score, model or build their next project, and employers looking for an experienced creative to join their team. Both will find the same things here, in one place: the work, the skills, and the way he works, without piecing them together from a dozen different platforms.

About tells his story: where he comes from, how he started and what he stands for. Portfolio collects his work across web, graphic design, AI-assisted production, 3D and media, with links to the projects themselves. Services sets out what he offers, from design, video and audio to 3D, AI workflows, app prototypes and creative direction. Social links to his public channels.

The site itself is one of his projects and part of the portfolio: the 3D plaques, the animated background and the light and dark designs were all made for it. Use the switch at the top right to change between them.

If you have a project or a role in mind, the Contact page is the quickest way to reach him, in English or German.
